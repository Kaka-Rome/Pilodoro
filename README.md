# Pilodoro
Pilodoro is a focus timer that helps you structure work and rest into manageable sessions. Start a timer, focus on one task, and take a short break when it’s time.

The interface is designed to stay out of your way, so you can keep your attention on what you’re doing.

## Features
- Set your own focus, short break, and long break durations.
- Keep a timer running while you use other apps.
- Review your focus history and statistics.
- Personalize the app with themes, colors, fonts, and wallpapers.
- Export and restore your settings and session history with a backup file.

## Local by design
Pilodoro does not require an account for its core features. Your settings and focus history are stored on your device. You can also create a manual backup if you want to save or move your data.

## About the name
Pilodoro combines the pill-shaped timer indicator with the Pomodoro technique. It reflects the app’s simple rhythm: focus for a while, then take a break.
